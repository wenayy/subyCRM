What's live
🔐 Auth LIVE
Google OAuth login. Access is restricted to allowed emails — only you can log in by default. Each user gets their own isolated data (contacts, inbox, notes, etc.).
👥 Contacts LIVE
Add, edit, search and filter contacts. Tag them, add notes, log interactions, set reminders, track relationship strength. Import via CSV. AI auto-classifies type and domain. Enrich any contact to auto-find their LinkedIn and Twitter/X via web search.
📥 Unified Inbox LIVE
All your messages from all platforms in one place. Real-time push via SSE — new messages appear instantly without refresh. Reply directly from the inbox. Works on mobile (tap conversation → full thread → back button).
🎙 Voice Bot (Telegram) LIVE
Send a voice note to @subyassistant_bot on Telegram. It transcribes with Whisper, extracts name/company/note with GPT, and creates or updates the contact automatically. Works anywhere — in a car, walking out of a meeting.
📧 Gmail LIVE
Connect in Settings → indexes your emails → matches them to CRM contacts → shows in inbox.
📅 Google Calendar LIVE
Sync calendar → upcoming meetings linked to contacts → pre-call view.
💬 WhatsApp LIVE
Scan QR in Settings (like WhatsApp Web). Incoming DMs from CRM contacts appear in inbox.
✈️ Telegram Personal LIVE
Connect with phone number. Reads personal DMs — not just bot messages. Syncs to inbox.
💼 Slack LIVE
OAuth connect. Syncs DMs every 5 minutes. Reply from inbox.
🎮 Discord LIVE
Bot token connect. Real-time messages from server members who are CRM contacts.
🏢 Companies LIVE
Company profiles with linked contacts. Enrichment auto-fills website, description, sector, funding stage.
📋 Sequences & Pipeline LIVE
Multi-step outreach sequences with delays. Pipeline board to track deals/relationships through stages.
🤖 Daily Brief LIVE
Every morning at 7:55 AM, the Telegram bot sends a brief: stale contacts to follow up, upcoming meetings, reminders due today.
Getting started (5 minutes)
1
Log in at suby-crm.vercel.app
Click "Continue with Google". Your email must be added to the allowed list in Railway → AUTH_ALLOWED_EMAILS.
2
Add your first contacts
Go to Contacts → Add manually, or import a CSV. After adding, hit "Enrich" on any contact to auto-find their LinkedIn and Twitter.
3
Connect your messaging platforms
Settings → connect Gmail, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack. Once connected, messages from your CRM contacts start appearing in Inbox.
4
Try the voice bot
Open Telegram → search @subyassistant_bot → send a voice note like "Just met Sarah Chen from Acme, she's the CTO, remind me to send her a deck tomorrow". Contact gets created automatically.
Known limitations right now
  • Twitter/X DM sync — requires a paid Twitter API plan (Basic $100/mo). Currently shows 402 error in logs. Read-only enrichment (finding handles) works fine.
  • LinkedIn DMs — not supported. LinkedIn blocks automated access. LinkedIn profile enrichment (finding profiles via web search) works fine.
  • Enrich accuracy — works best when contact has company + role filled in. Common names with no context may return wrong person.
  • WhatsApp — uses WhatsApp Web protocol (Baileys). Session may disconnect occasionally and needs re-scan.
